About micro1

micro1 is a data lab that helps AI labs and enterprises train foundational models and agents. Its work includes frontier evaluations, reinforcement-learning environments and contextual evaluations designed to improve large language models and enterprise AI agents.

Its AI recruiting platform sources and vets domain experts who contribute specialized, high-quality training data across fields such as law, medicine, finance, coding and mathematics.

About the Role

micro1 is seeking highly skilled Mathematics Experts for an AI training project involving advanced mathematical problem-solving, computational mathematics, numerical methods and Python.

You will create, solve, review and validate mathematical tasks using reproducible, programmatic and command-line-based workflows.

What You’ll Work On

  • Solve and validate advanced mathematical and computational problems.
  • Translate mathematical requirements into precise, executable workflows.
  • Implement mathematical methods using Python and command-line tools.
  • Automate calculations, numerical experiments, parameter sweeps and visualizations.
  • Test special cases and verify mathematical and numerical results.

Required Qualifications

  • MS or PhD in Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Operations Research, Computational Science, Theoretical Computer Science or a closely related quantitative field; or
  • BS in Mathematics or a related quantitative field plus at least 1–2 years of mathematics-focused professional or research experience at leading companies or institutions.
  • Strong expertise in at least one area such as numerical analysis, optimization, probability, statistics, discrete mathematics, algebra, geometry, mathematical modeling or dynamical systems.
