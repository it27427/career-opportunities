Job Overview

This Software and Data Engineer position is available within the University of Maryland’s Division of Information Technology (DIT), in the Mid-Atlantic Crossroads (MAX) department.

The role works closely with network engineers and global DNS root operators to develop and support software, network management, service support, and infrastructure that improve the productivity and reliability of the MAX network and support DNS “D” root services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, plan, and implement well-engineered software, network management, and service-support solutions.
  • Support documentation and existing C-language code.
  • Support and expand the DNS root server “D” in collaboration with global root operators.
  • Establish standard, repeatable solutions for common software and data requirements.
  • Work regularly with network engineers to build software utilities and tools that improve network productivity and reliability.
  • Participate in conference calls, email discussions, and operations meetings with DNS root operators.
  • Discuss and help reconcile disruptions in the global DNS environment.
  • Assist with support of the MAX server environment that supports the MAX network.

Minimum Qualifications

Education: Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university.

Experience: Three (3) years of professional experience in network design, research, implementation, and escalation support.

Education substitution: Additional work experience as defined above may be substituted year-for-year for up to four (4) years of the required education.

Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities

  • Knowledge of core network design and implementation over WAN.
  • Knowledge of scalable networks.
  • Oral and written communication skills.
  • Skill in using Google Workspace.
  • Ability to interpret and apply policies, procedures, regulations, and laws.
  • Ability to multitask while maintaining a commitment to customer service.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive knowledge of DNS.
  • Ability to support existing C-language code.
  • Server management support experience.
  • Ciena and Cisco networking experience.
  • Knowledge of fiber optics, outside plant (OSP), and fiber-optic testing using OTDR.

Work Schedule and Physical Requirements

  • This position has an in-person component, with expected weekly attendance at the College Park, Maryland location. Telework is not guaranteed.
  • Evening, night, weekend, or different-shift work may be required for extended periods.
  • Weekends and evenings may be needed based on operational requirements.
  • Occasional racking and stacking of servers and network equipment is required, with a maximum lifting limit of 50 pounds.
  • Must be able to walk short distances and drive a state vehicle.
  • A valid driver’s license is required to operate a state vehicle.
Essential position: During a major catastrophic event, weather emergency, or other operational emergency, the employee may be required to work at the normal work location or an alternative location to help maintain continuity of University services.

Visa Sponsorship and Employment Eligibility

Visa sponsorship: The Division of Information Technology will not sponsor the successful candidate for U.S. work authorization now or in the future. F-1 STEM OPT support is not available for this position.

Employment eligibility: The successful candidate must complete Form I-9 employment eligibility verification by presenting documents establishing identity and work authorization within the required federal timeframe. Where applicable, renewed work authorization must also be demonstrated.

Failure to complete employment eligibility verification or reverification within the required timeframe may result in suspension or termination of employment.
