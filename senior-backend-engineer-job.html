About the opportunity

We're partnering with a large enterprise technology organization to place a Senior Software Engineer on a platform team that powers core customer-facing experiences across web and mobile.

The team collaborates across Engineering, UX, Product, Business, and Analytics to ship quickly, learn through experimentation and customer feedback, and integrate advanced capabilities such as Generative AI into production services.

This is a full-time commitment of 40 hours per week. The listing describes the position as W-2 employment with Cincinnatus LLC, with the opportunity to be placed within an enterprise client's engineering organization.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead delivery of scoped features with collaborative engineering teams, aligned with platform and system objectives.
  • Design, develop, test, and maintain efficient services in Node.js, TypeScript, and Rust across the software lifecycle and CI/CD.
  • Build and optimize server-to-server integrations and REST and GraphQL API layers for high-traffic applications.
  • Integrate AI/ML capabilities into product services and use generative AI tools to improve engineering productivity.
  • Contribute to solution design, code reviews, and scalable, secure, maintainable engineering practices.
  • Monitor performance, resolve defects, drive continuous improvement, and mentor other engineers.

Core qualifications

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, or a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field and 3+ years of experience.
  • Hands-on production backend coding experience with Node.js and TypeScript.
  • Hands-on Rust experience in production services, performance-critical modules, or substantial systems work.
  • Strong web fundamentals: HTTP semantics, cookies, headers, caching, sessions, service authentication, timeouts, and retries.
  • Experience building or operating high-traffic, consumer-facing web platforms.
  • Reliable weekday availability for at least 40 hours per week, with strong written and verbal communication skills.
