Career tips that actually help

Short, practical advice to strengthen every application.

Tailor your resume to each role

Mirror the keywords in the job description and lead with results, not duties. Recruiters skim, so make impact obvious in the first few lines.

Prepare stories, not scripts

For interviews, prepare three or four real examples that show problem-solving, teamwork and ownership. Structure them as situation, action, result.

Apply early, follow up politely

Early applicants are often reviewed first. A short follow-up after a week shows genuine interest without being pushy.

Verify before you share details

Legitimate employers do not ask for payment to apply. Use official channels and be cautious with personal information.
